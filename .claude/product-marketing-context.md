## Who Eva Is

Eva Knudsen is a Norwegian woman in her mid-40s who has lived in Cotignac, a small village in Provence, France, for 12 years. She is **not** a doctor, nutritionist, or certified health professional.

Her authority comes from **lived observation**: over 12 years she has watched women in her village — neighbours, in-laws, friends' mothers — in their 50s, 60s and 70s stay leaner, more energetic and healthier than most Norwegian women she knows, without diets, calorie counting, or medication.

She has studied what they do differently and built a framework around it: **Provence-prinsippet™**.

**Eva's voice:** Warm, direct, personal, evidence-aware but not clinical. She writes in first person. No wellness fluff. No sensationalism. Honest about what she is and isn't.

## The Product — Provence-prinsippet™ Webinar

### What she teaches in 60 minutes

1. **The biological frame** — What actually happens in the female body at 45+ (hormonal transition), and why everything that worked before stops working. Validates the experience without blaming the woman.

2. **Provence-prinsippet™** — Four building blocks for each meal, drawn from real Provence farmhouse cooking (not "Norwegian Mediterranean diet"). Specifically calibrated for brain, heart, energy, weight and hot flashes during perimenopause/menopause.

3. **One meal to make tonight** — 5 ingredients, 25 minutes. Concrete and immediately actionable.

### What it is NOT
- Not a diet
- Not calorie counting or macro tracking
- Not Wegovy or any medication
- Not a strict plan
- Not stock "Mediterranean diet" advice

## Target Audience

### Primary: Norwegian women 45–58 in «den perfekte stormen»

The "perfect storm" is the convergence of hormonal transition symptoms:

| Symptom | How they describe it |
|---|---|
| Weight gain | "4–9 kilo de siste 3–5 årene uten å gjøre noe annerledes" |
| Brain fog | "Hjernetåke, særlig om ettermiddagen" |
| Energy crash | "Energien forsvinner kl 15 og kommer ikke tilbake" |
| Sleep | "Søvn som svikter 2–3 netter i uka" |
| Hot flashes | "Hetetokter, eller en uforklarlig varme" |
| Diet fatigue | "Prøvd 2+ dietter siden 2020 — de funket, til de ikke funket" |
| Medication ambivalence | "Har vurdert Wegovy, eller er på det og vurderer å slutte" |

**Key insight:** These women blame themselves (willpower, discipline). The reframe is: it is biology, not character failure.

**Approx. market size:** ~140,000 Norwegian women living this right now.

### Ideal customer profile

- Female, 45–58
- Norwegian, living in Norway
- Has tried at least 2 diets in the last 5 years
- Trusts science but is frustrated that no doctor has talked concretely about food with her
- Has resources to invest in herself — wants something adult and lasting, not a quick fix
- Is NOT looking for "10 kg in 4 weeks"
- Is NOT interested in calorie schemas or macro tracking

### Who it is NOT for
- Women wanting rapid weight loss
- Women seeking the fastest fix
- Women who want a strict structured plan
